PayPackage, KPI, deductions, holiday multiplier
KPI bonus applies after probation

Use the KPI amount from your last payslip, not a guess.

1 = calendar month. If your payroll runs from the 21st to the 20th, enter 21.

The holiday multiplier applies to the base hourly rate. The night allowance is added on top, unmultiplied.

PayslipsMake the estimates match your real pay

Enter each payslip you receive. The app learns your real deductions, and for months you tracked fully it learns how far its estimate was from your real pay. It uses that to adjust "expected" earnings.

Gross + net saves the payslip and sets your real deduction %. Base sets the package. KPI sets the bonus.

With this on, the shift starts, pauses for lunch and ends by itself at the scheduled times. Open the app any time and it catches up. Days you did not work: tap the day in the Calendar and mark it off.

Days off & holidaysRotating days off, public holidays

Public holidays

Built in: Jan 7, Jan 25, Apr 25, May 1, Jun 30, Jul 23, Oct 6. Eid, Sham El-Nessim and other moving holidays are not built in. Add them when announced, or tap the day in the Calendar. Working a holiday pays the multiplier. By default a holiday is a day off.

AlertsNotifications and phone alarms

A web page cannot wake a locked phone. In-app alerts work while the app is open. The .ics file adds real calendar alarms for the next 14 days (shift start, breaks, shift end) that ring with the screen locked. Download it again after changing your schedule.

DataBackup, automatic copies, transfer

Everything is stored on this device only. Use Transfer to copy your data to your phone or PC as text.
